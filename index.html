Leila Shah had her life all planned—until one reckless night changed everything.

Notorious bachelor and captain of the Vancouver Vanguards, Ragnar Colton, has one rule: never mix ice and spice. That changes the night he's set to face off against his backstabbing ex-teammate and spots Leila Shah wearing his jersey, unable to take his eyes off her.

Spurred by a dare, Leila finds herself in Ragnar's arms after their intimate date—then flees, humiliated, vowing to put the night behind her. Six weeks later, a positive pregnancy test disrupts her plans to forget him. And as cryptic warnings suggest Ragnar may not be the man he seems, Leila must navigate a maze of deceit where every choice carries a consequence.

The Author

Meet Rebecca

Welcome, I’m so glad you’re here!

I’m Rebecca, and I write slow-burn, high-angst hockey romance: grudges, grovels, and love stories that make you want to throw the book across the room (in a good way). I believe in second chances that have to be earned, heroes who bleed for it, and heroines who don’t fold.

My books are rooted in Canada, from Vancouver to the mountains of Whistler, where professional hockey collides with close-knit communities and everybody’s business has a way of becoming everybody else’s.

I live in British Columbia and hold a BA in Publishing and an MA in Creative Writing. When I’m not writing, I’m usually at a live show, trying a new class, dancing, or reminding my tiny chorkie, Freya, that she is, in fact, the cutest.
